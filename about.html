OUR MISSION

Care, with a
side of community.

FoodShare connects food donors—like restaurants, grocers, and neighbours—with local people and organisations who can use fresh surplus food. Our goal is simple: less food wasted, more people cared for.

We help donors list what's available, let receivers request what they need, and make pickup arrangements clear and local.
